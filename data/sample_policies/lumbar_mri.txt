Sample Coverage Policy SAMPLE-LMRI-001
Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) of the Lumbar Spine
Applies to CPT 72148 (MRI lumbar spine without contrast material)
Fictional Example Health Plan - Effective date: January 1, 2026

Purpose
This sample policy describes when an MRI of the lumbar spine is considered reasonable
and necessary. It was written for a demonstration of decision-support software and is
loosely modeled on the structure of public Medicare local coverage determinations. It
must not be used for real coverage decisions.

Background
Most episodes of acute low back pain improve within six weeks with conservative care.
Early imaging without warning signs rarely changes treatment and may lead to unnecessary
procedures. Imaging is therefore reserved for patients whose symptoms persist despite an
adequate trial of conservative therapy, or who show warning signs ("red flags") of serious
underlying disease.

=== Page 2 ===
Coverage Indications

Section A - Clinical indication (at least ONE of the following must be met)

A1. Persistent symptoms after conservative therapy. Low back pain or lumbar radiculopathy
has lasted at least 6 weeks AND the patient has completed at least 6 weeks of conservative
therapy without adequate improvement. Conservative therapy includes physical therapy, a home
exercise program directed by a clinician, non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) or
other analgesics, and activity modification. The duration and type of therapy must be
documented in the medical record.

A2. Red flag findings. Imaging may be performed without a trial of conservative therapy when
any red flag is documented: progressive or severe neurological deficit (for example new
foot drop or worsening leg weakness), symptoms of cauda equina syndrome (new bowel or bladder
dysfunction, saddle anesthesia), suspected spinal infection, suspected malignancy (history of
cancer with new back pain, unexplained weight loss), or suspected fracture after significant
trauma.

Section B - Documentation (ALL of the following must be met)

B1. A physical examination including a neurological examination of the lower extremities
(for example strength, reflexes, sensation, or straight leg raise) is documented within the
30 days before the request.

Limitations
Repeat MRI of the lumbar spine within 12 months is not covered unless there is a significant
change in symptoms or a new red flag.

Coding Information - ICD-10-CM codes that support medical necessity
M54.50  Low back pain, unspecified
M54.16  Radiculopathy, lumbar region
M54.17  Radiculopathy, lumbosacral region
M51.16  Intervertebral disc disorders with radiculopathy, lumbar region
M48.061 Spinal stenosis, lumbar region without neurogenic claudication
G83.4   Cauda equina syndrome

Documentation Requirements
The medical record must contain the duration of symptoms, the conservative therapy tried
(type and duration), any red flag findings, and the physical examination findings.
