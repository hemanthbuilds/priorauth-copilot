Orthopedic Clinic Note - Right knee (synthetic patient)

68-year-old with right knee osteoarthritis. Standing X-rays of the right knee show severe medial joint space narrowing, large periarticular osteophytes and subchondral sclerosis. Pain is 8/10 and she cannot walk more than one block, needs a railing to climb stairs, and pain wakes her at night.

Conservative care over the past 6 months: supervised physical therapy for 12 weeks, daily meloxicam, and two corticosteroid injections, all without lasting relief. She uses a cane.

No signs of infection in the knee, no open wounds, no urinary or dental infection, and no neurological disease.

Plan: right total knee arthroplasty.
